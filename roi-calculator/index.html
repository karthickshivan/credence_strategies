WHY THE NUMBERS MOVE

What changes your return in the real world.

Property Type

Land Earns More Than a Flat

Developed land and commercial property generally compound faster over time. A flat earns steadily. The right choice depends on how long you want to wait and how much you want to be involved.

Management

An Empty Flat Earns Nothing

Occupancy is everything. A well managed property with reliable tenants and quick repairs almost always outperforms one left to look after itself. Management is not a cost — it is the return.

Time

Longer Horizons Compound Faster

A property held for fifteen years and managed properly will almost always outperform one bought and sold in three. Move the horizon slider and watch the gap grow.
