About Credence Strategies

Building trust, delivering excellence since 2004

Our Story

From Vision to Reality

Founded in 2004 by Rajiv Menon, Credence Strategies began with a simple yet powerful vision: to bring professional, transparent, and trustworthy property management to South India's growing real estate market.

What started as a small operation in Bangalore has grown into South India's most trusted real estate asset management company, serving over 4,000 clients across 15 countries and managing assets worth over ₹2,000 crores.

Our commitment to excellence, combined with cutting-edge technology and deep market expertise, has made us the preferred choice for NRI investors and HNI clients worldwide.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative real estate asset management company in India, empowering property owners worldwide to maximize their investments through professional management, cutting-edge technology, and unparalleled service excellence.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional returns and peace of mind to our clients through professional property management, transparent operations, and data-driven investment strategies. We are committed to transforming real estate ownership from a burden into a source of reliable, passive income.
