FAQ · Referral Program

Questions Before You Refer Someone

Short, honest answers.

Who can I refer to Credence?
You can refer anyone who owns property in India and could benefit from trusted property management services. This includes NRIs, friends, family members, colleagues, or local property owners with residential, commercial, or land assets. There is no limit to the number of people you can refer.
When do I get my referral reward?
You receive your referral reward once the person you referred successfully completes their onboarding and becomes a Credence client. For every successful referral, you receive one month of free property management, and your friend also receives a welcome benefit. We keep you informed throughout the process, so you'll always know the status of your reward.
Is my friend's information kept private?
Yes. We use the information you share only to make a single, professional, no-pressure introduction. We never add your friend to marketing lists, share their details with third parties, or continue contacting them if they are not interested. If they decline, we respect their decision and end the conversation immediately.
